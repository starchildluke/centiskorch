Flareon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon, introduced in Generation 1. It is one of the Eeveelutions.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Flareon is a four-legged Pokémon with reddish orange fur and a cream-coloured bushy tail, fluffy collar, and tuft of hair on its head. Its ears are pointy and has slightly longer fur than on the rest of its body.

When it gets excited, it can reach temperatures of around 927°C (1,700°F).

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
