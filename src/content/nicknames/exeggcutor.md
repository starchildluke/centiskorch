Exeggutor is a dual Grass/Psychic-type, introduced in Generation 1. It has an Alolan form which is dual Grass/Dragon-type. It evolves from Exeggcute with a Leaf Stone.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Exeggutor is a bipedal Pokémon that looks like a tropical tree (perhaps a coconut tree or palm tree). It has a brown body and no arms and feet with two toes on each. The Alolan form has a very long neck, attributed to abundant growth in the Alolan sun.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
* Nicknames exclusive to the Alolan form will be marked with an asterisk
* Although Exeggutor is based on a coconut tree, some nicknames will reference eggs given its pre-evolution and the idea that you probably caught it as an Exeggcute.

With all that said, let's look at those Exeggutor nicknames!
