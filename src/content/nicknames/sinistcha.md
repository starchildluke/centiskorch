Sinistcha is a dual Grass/Ghost-type Pokémon, introduced in Generation 9. It's made of green matcha tea with thin arms are and two small legs coming out of the bottom of the cup it lives in. It has a matcha whisk on its head.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C with S being the best.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
