Zigzagoon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon, introduced in Generation 3. It has a Galarian form, introduced in Generation 8 which is a dual Dark/Normal-type.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Zigzagoon is a raccoon-like Pokémon with dark beige/brown zigzag-patterned fur. It has a blindfold-style pattern across its brown eyes. The Galarian form is black and white and more friendly and playful, akin to a dog.

Its name derives from the zig zag pattern of its fur and its likeness to raccoons and raccoon dogs.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
