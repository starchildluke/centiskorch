Porygon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon, introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Porygon2 when traded while holding an Upgrade and finally Porygon-Z when traded while holding a Dubious Disc.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Porygon is an duck/paper crane-like Pokémon made out code. Its body is polyhedral with a pink head and blue nose, blue legs and tail and a mainly pink torso. It has two hexagonal eyes.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
* These nicknames should also work for its evolutions.
* Thanks to [Jake](https://www.twitch.tv/heyboyblue) for the suggestion!

With all that said, let's look at those Porygon nicknames!
