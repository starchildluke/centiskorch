Steelix is a dual Steel/Ground type Pokémon, introduced in Generation 2 as the evolution of Onix. It also has a Mega Evolution.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Steelix is a metallic serpentine Pokémon with a helmet-like head and a body made up of silvery metallic boulders. It has an overbite and red irises. The Mega Evolution has metallic shards orbiting its body and iridescent crystals coming out of its body as well as blue-outlined hexagons on some of the boulders. It's jaw is smoother and has three shards coming out of each side.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
* Nicknames marked with an asterisk were courtesy of [SalarezArt](https://ko-fi.com/salarezart)
