Gardevoir is a dual Psychic/Fairy-type, introduced in Generation 3 (as a pure Psychic type). It also has a Mega form and evolves from Kirlia at Level 30.

Let's have a look at its origins and some of my suggested nicknames.

## Origins

Gardevoir is a bipedal Pokémon and its body looks a flowing white gown. Its hair and arms are green as well as the underside of its gown. It has red eyes and red fin-like plate on its chest and back. Its hair covers over part of its face, exposing one eye.

## Nicknames

My methodology for nicknames is as follows:

* I rank nicknames by lettered tiers: S, A, B, and C. S is the best and D is the worst.
* I'll usually list my inspiration for a nickname so you know where they came from.
* Nicknames exclusive to the Alolan form will be marked with an asterisk

With all that said, let's look at those Gardevoir nicknames!
