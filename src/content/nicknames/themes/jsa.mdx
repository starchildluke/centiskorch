The Justice Society of America (JSA) is a superhero team similar to the Justice League of America, as part of the DC Universe. Comic book characters make cool nicknames for Pokémon and given the super powers and elemental abilities, it's a match made in heaven.

## Nicknames

My methodology for picking JSA-themed nicknames is the same as [Golden Sun](/nicknames/themes/golden-sun/) so go read it there! This is also an imperfect list. If you have suggestions for inclusions, [let me know](/contact-me/)!

* **Wonder Woman**: Tsareena, Braviary, any Pokémon that learns Vine Whip or Power Whip (e.g. [Venusaur](/nicknames/venusaur/), [Tangrowth](/nicknames/tangrowth/), [Ferrothorn](/nicknames/ferrothorn/), Centiskorch!), Aegislash, Tinkatink line, [Gardevoir](/nicknames/gardevoir/), Rapidash, Keldeo, Zamazenta, Zacian, Tapu Lele, Hatterene
* **The Flash**: Jolteon, Electrike line, Blitzle line, Yamper line, Pawmi line, Regieleki, Zeraora, Accelgor, [Deoxys (Speed)](/nicknames/deoxys/), Electrode, Dragapult, [Miraidon](/nicknames/miraidon/)
* **GreenLantern**: Reuniclus, Gardevoir, Gallade, Espeon (shiny), Zygarde, [Rayquaza](/nicknames/rayquaza/), Virizion, Iron Leaves, Celebi, Decidueye
* **Hawkgirl / Hawkman**: Any bird Pokémon (Pidgeot, Fearow, Noctowl, Swellow, Staraptor, Honchkrow, Braviary, Mandibuzz, Talonflame, Hawlucha, Kilowattrel), Skarmory, Corviknight, Archeops, [Aerodactyl](/nicknames/aerodactyl/), Tinkaton
